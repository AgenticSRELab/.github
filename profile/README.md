## Agentic SRE Lab

At the **Agentic SRE Lab**, we are pioneering the transition from traditional automation to cognitive autonomy in Site Reliability Engineering. As modern infrastructure grows increasingly complex, static scripts and manual runbooks are no longer sufficient to maintain system stability.

Our research focuses on the development of **Agentic systems**—autonomous AI agents capable of reasoning, planning, and executing complex SRE tasks. We look beyond simple automation to create systems that can "think" about reliability. From **self-healing incident response** and **proactive observability** to safe **change management**, our mission is to design the next generation of resilient systems where human operators are architects of reliability, not just maintainers of it.


Our core research pillars include:

* **Next-Gen Observability:** Moving from passive monitoring to active, investigative agents that detect anomalies and contextually analyze root causes.
* **Autonomous Incident Response:** Developing agents capable of triaging, mitigating, and resolving live incidents with minimal human intervention.
* **Intelligent Change Management:** utilizing agents to predict the blast radius of deployments and manage rollbacks dynamically.
* **Reliable System Design:** Researching architectures that are natively designed to interact with and support agentic maintenance.
